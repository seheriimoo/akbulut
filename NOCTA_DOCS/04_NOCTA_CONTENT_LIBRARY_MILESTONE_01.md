# MISSING SOMEONE

## INSIGHT 001 — Thinking About Them Again

### Core Insight

Thoughts often return to emotionally significant people.

Returning thoughts do not always indicate unfinished love.

The mind naturally revisits what once mattered.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 001

Sometimes the person is not the only thing being missed.

It may also be the version of yourself that existed around them.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 002

When someone has been part of your daily life for a long time, the mind can miss the familiarity as much as the person.

Not every return of thought is a return of love.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 003

Some memories return not because they belong in the future.

But because they once carried a strong emotional charge.

The mind often revisits what mattered.

---

## INSIGHT 002 — Losing The Future You Imagined

### Core Insight

Sometimes what is missed is not only the person.

It is the future that once felt possible with them.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 001

Sometimes what hurts is not losing the person.

It is losing the future you had already begun to build around them.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 002

The mind does not only grieve what happened.

It can also grieve the moments, plans and memories it expected to happen one day.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 003

Perhaps what feels difficult to let go of is not the relationship itself.

It may be the life you imagined it would become.
