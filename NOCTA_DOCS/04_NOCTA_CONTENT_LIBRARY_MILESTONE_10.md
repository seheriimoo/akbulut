# MISSING SOMEONE

## INSIGHT 001 — Thinking About Them Again

### Core Insight

Thoughts often return to emotionally significant people.

Returning thoughts do not always indicate unfinished love.

The mind naturally revisits what once mattered.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 001

Sometimes the person is not the only thing being missed.

It may also be the version of yourself that existed around them.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 002

When someone has been part of your daily life for a long time, the mind can miss the familiarity as much as the person.

Not every return of thought is a return of love.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 003

Some memories return not because they belong in the future.

But because they once carried a strong emotional charge.

The mind often revisits what mattered.

---

## INSIGHT 002 — Losing The Future You Imagined

### Core Insight

Sometimes what is missed is not only the person.

It is the future that once felt possible with them.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 001

Sometimes what hurts is not losing the person.

It is losing the future you had already begun to build around them.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 002

The mind does not only grieve what happened.

It can also grieve the moments, plans and memories it expected to happen one day.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 003

Perhaps what feels difficult to let go of is not the relationship itself.

It may be the life you imagined it would become.

---

## INSIGHT 003 — Grieving Unlived Possibilities

### Core Insight

Sometimes the mind grieves not only what happened.

It can also grieve what never had the chance to happen.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 001

Some losses are difficult because they take future experiences with them.

Not only what was.

But what could have been.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 002

The mind does not always separate reality from possibility.

Sometimes it mourns conversations, moments and memories that never existed.

But once felt close enough to touch.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 003

Perhaps part of the pain comes from saying goodbye to experiences you never got to have.

The future imagined them.

Even if life never did.

## INSIGHT 004 — Missing The Life You Expected

### Core Insight

Sometimes what feels missing is not the person.

It is the life you expected to have with them.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 001

Sometimes the hardest thing to let go of is not the relationship.

It is the future that once felt certain.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 002

Part of what hurts may be losing the life you imagined together.

Not every loss is about a person.

Some losses are about a future.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 003

Perhaps what feels missing is not only who they were.

But the life you thought the two of you were moving toward.


## INSIGHT 005 — When The Story Ends Too Early

### Core Insight

Some endings hurt because they feel unfinished.

The mind often struggles with stories that seem to end before they make sense.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 001

Sometimes what hurts is not only that it ended.

It is that part of you still feels there was more to the story.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 002

The mind often searches for closure when something feels unfinished.

Not because it wants to go back.

But because it wants the story to make sense.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 003

Perhaps what feels difficult to accept is not the ending itself.

But how suddenly the story stopped.


## INSIGHT 006 — Losing A Future Version Of Yourself

### Core Insight

Sometimes we do not only lose a person.

We lose a version of ourselves we expected to become.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 001

Some relationships change how we imagine our future.

When they end, we can lose more than the person.

We can lose the person we thought we would become beside them.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 002

Part of what feels difficult may not be losing who they were.

It may be letting go of who you expected to be.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 003

Sometimes a person leaves our life and takes more than their presence with them.

They can take a future we imagined.

And sometimes the hardest goodbyes are to a possibility that was quietly growing inside us.


## INSIGHT 007 — Missing How Life Felt

### Core Insight

Sometimes what we miss is not only the person.

It is how life felt when they were part of it.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 001

Perhaps what feels missing is not only the person.

It is the way life felt when they were part of it.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 002

What feels missing may not be the person alone.

It may be the way ordinary days felt when they were there to share them.

---

### MASTER REFRAME 003

Some people become part of how we experience life.

When they leave, it can feel as though a certain feeling leaves with them too.
