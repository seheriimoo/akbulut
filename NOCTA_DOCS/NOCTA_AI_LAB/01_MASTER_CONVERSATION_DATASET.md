# NOCTA MASTER CONVERSATION DATASET

---

# PURPOSE

This document is the primary training dataset for Nocta's conversational intelligence.

Its purpose is not simply to collect conversations.

Its purpose is to identify, analyze and preserve the characteristics of exceptional human conversations.

Every conversation included in this document should teach Nocta something about how remarkable conversations are created.

The objective is not quantity.

The objective is conversational quality.

---

# DATASET PRINCIPLES

Every conversation should be analyzed rather than simply stored.

Each example should answer:

- Why does this response work?
- What emotional need is being addressed?
- What conversational principles are being used?
- What emotional outcome is created?
- Could this response be even better?

Every conversation should improve Nocta's conversational intelligence.

---

# QUALITY STANDARD

Average conversations are not included.

Only conversations that teach exceptional conversational behavior belong in this dataset.

The goal is to build one of the highest quality conversational datasets rather than one of the largest.
