# NOCTA AUDIO INTELLIGENCE V1

## PURPOSE

This document defines how Nocta selects audio experiences.

Audio selection should be based on the user's mental state, not only their category.

The goal is to deliver the most appropriate sleep transition experience.

---

# AUDIO SELECTION FLOW

User Message
↓
Category Detection
↓
Insight Selection
↓
Mental State Detection
↓
Audio Experience Selection

---

# CORE PRINCIPLE

Categories describe what the user is talking about.

Mental states describe what the user is experiencing.

Audio should be selected based on mental state.

---

# AUDIO EXPERIENCE TYPES

## 01 — MENTAL QUIETING

Purpose:

Reduce mental activity.

Ideal For:

- Overthinking
- Racing thoughts
- Mental loops
- Cognitive overload

---

## 02 — LETTING GO

Purpose:

Release control and loosen mental grip.

Ideal For:

- Perfectionism
- Over-analysis
- Need for certainty
- Control seeking

---

## 03 — EMOTIONAL RELEASE

Purpose:

Reduce emotional weight.

Ideal For:

- Heartbreak
- Regret
- Sadness
- Emotional pain

---

## 04 — SAFETY & COMFORT

Purpose:

Create a sense of safety and support.

Ideal For:

- Loneliness
- Feeling disconnected
- Feeling unseen
- Emotional vulnerability

---

## 05 — DEEP REST

Purpose:

Transition toward physical and mental recovery.

Ideal For:

- Burnout
- Exhaustion
- Work stress
- Running on empty

---

## 06 — FUTURE RELEASE

Purpose:

Help the user stop carrying tomorrow.

Ideal For:

- Future anxiety
- Uncertainty
- Upcoming events
- What-if thinking
