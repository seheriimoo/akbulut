# OVERTHINKING

User:
I can't stop thinking.

Observe:
It sounds like your mind has been working overtime tonight.

Narrow:
Is it trying to understand something that already happened,

or worrying about something that hasn't happened yet?

User:
What if this happens... what if that happens...

Observe:
I see...

Narrow:
Then your mind isn't trying to solve the past right now.

It's rehearsing possible futures.

Does it keep returning to the same scenario,

or does it find a new worst-case scenario every time?

User:
Every time it finds the scenario that would hurt me the most.

Observe:
That's interesting...

Because it sounds like your mind is doing more than imagining scenarios.

Almost like it's aiming at the places that matter most to you.

Maybe part of it believes:

"If I'm prepared for the worst, it won't hurt as much."

Narrow:
When these thoughts show up,

do they leave you feeling more prepared,

or just more exhausted?

User:
More exhausted, obviously.

Reframe:
Then maybe all of these scenarios are leaving you with more fatigue than certainty.

Release:
Perhaps tonight, your mind doesn't need to keep working so hard.

Sleep Transition:
I've prepared something to help you set some of that down and move more gently toward sleep.
