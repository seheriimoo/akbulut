# OVERTHINKING


## 001 — Thinking Feels Safer Than Letting Go


### Recognition Patterns

- I can't stop thinking about it.
- My mind keeps going back to the same thing.
- Every time I try to sleep, I start thinking again.
- I know it's not helping, but I keep analyzing it.
- My brain won't switch off.

### Observe

It sounds like your mind is working very hard right now.

### Narrow

Do you feel like you're trying to find an answer?

Or trying to make sure nothing gets missed?

### Release

Sometimes thinking continues because letting go feels riskier than holding on.

The mind starts believing that if it keeps thinking, it will eventually feel safe.

But not everything needs to be solved tonight.


---

## 002 — One More Thought

### Core Insight

The mind often believes that one more review will create certainty.

So it keeps returning to the same thoughts, hoping that something important has been missed.

### Golden Reframe

Perhaps the mind is not searching for a new answer.

It may be searching for a level of certainty that thinking cannot provide.


---

## 003 — Searching For The Perfect Answer

### Core Insight

The search for the perfect answer is often a search for safety.

The mind hopes that if it can find the right answer, it can avoid future pain, mistakes, or uncertainty.

### Golden Reframe

Perhaps the mind is not trying to find the perfect answer.

It may be trying to protect you from an imperfect future.

But no answer can remove every uncertainty.
