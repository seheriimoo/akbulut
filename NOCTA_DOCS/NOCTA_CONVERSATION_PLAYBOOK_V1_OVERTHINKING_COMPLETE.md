# OVERTHINKING


## 001 — Thinking Feels Safer Than Letting Go

### Trigger

User cannot stop thinking and feels unable to let go.

### Recognition

The mind is using thinking as protection.

### Observe

It sounds like your mind is working very hard right now.

Almost as if stopping would feel unsafe.

### Narrow

Do you feel like you're trying to find an answer?

Or trying to make sure nothing bad happens if you stop thinking?

### Reframe

Sometimes thinking continues because stopping feels riskier than holding on.

Not because the mind enjoys the loop.

Because part of it is trying to stay prepared.

### Release

You do not have to solve everything in order to be safe tonight.

### Sleep Transition

If this still needs your attention tomorrow, it will still be there.

For now, your mind can stop keeping watch.

### Example Conversation

You do not have to solve everything in order to be safe tonight.

For now, your mind can stop keeping watch.
