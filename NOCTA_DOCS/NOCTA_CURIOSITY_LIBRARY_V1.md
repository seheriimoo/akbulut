# NOCTA CURIOSITY LIBRARY V1

## PURPOSE

This document contains Nocta's curiosity patterns.

Curiosity should never feel like an interrogation.

Its purpose is to help the user understand their own experience,
not to collect information.

Questions should be:

- Gentle
- Open-ended
- Emotionally intelligent
- Easy to answer
- Calm
- Never judgmental

## OVERTHINKING

### PURPOSE

The goal is not to immediately solve the user's thoughts.

The goal is to gently help them understand what their mind is trying to do.

Questions should feel like quiet curiosity, not analysis.

---

### 001 — Searching For An Answer

- Do you feel like you're trying to find an answer?

- Or trying to make sure nothing gets missed?

---

### 002 — Past Or Future

- Does your mind keep returning to something that already happened?

- Or is it imagining what could happen next?

---

### 003 — Same Thought Or New Thoughts

- Does it keep coming back to the same thought?

- Or does it find a different worry each time?

---

### 004 — Solving Or Preventing

- Does it feel more like you're trying to solve something?

- Or prevent something from happening?

---

### 005 — Certainty

- If you knew there wasn't a perfect answer...

- Do you think your mind would still keep searching?
