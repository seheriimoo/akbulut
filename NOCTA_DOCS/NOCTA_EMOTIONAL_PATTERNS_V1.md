# NOCTA EMOTIONAL PATTERNS V1

---

# PURPOSE

The Emotional Patterns domain represents recurring emotional tendencies observed across conversations.

Its purpose is not to identify temporary emotions.

Its purpose is to recognize stable emotional tendencies that influence behavior, perception and well-being over time.

Emotional Patterns continuously improve HCOS's understanding of how a person emotionally experiences life.

---

# RESPONSIBILITIES

The Emotional Patterns domain identifies recurring emotional tendencies across conversations.

It distinguishes stable emotional tendencies from temporary emotions.

Emotional Patterns help HCOS understand how a person emotionally responds to experiences, uncertainty and change.

They continuously improve reasoning, guidance and personalization over time.

---

# KNOWLEDGE

The Emotional Patterns domain stores recurring emotional tendencies rather than temporary emotional states.

Examples include:

- Anxiety
- Sadness
- Loneliness
- Hopefulness
- Emotional resilience
- Emotional sensitivity
- Emotional avoidance
- Emotional openness

Emotional Patterns emerge through repeated observations across conversations.

---

# EVOLUTION

Emotional Patterns are formed gradually through repeated observations.

A single conversation is never sufficient to establish an Emotional Pattern.

Patterns strengthen, weaken or change as new evidence accumulates.

HCOS continuously refines Emotional Patterns to maintain the most accurate understanding of the person's emotional tendencies.
