# NOCTA MENTAL PATTERNS V1

---

# PURPOSE

The Mental Patterns domain represents recurring ways of thinking observed across conversations.

Its purpose is not to classify isolated thoughts.

Its purpose is to recognize stable cognitive tendencies that influence reasoning, decision-making and perception over time.

Mental Patterns continuously improve HCOS's understanding of how a person thinks.

---

# RESPONSIBILITIES

The Mental Patterns domain identifies recurring ways of thinking across conversations.

It distinguishes stable cognitive tendencies from temporary thoughts.

Mental Patterns help HCOS understand how a person processes information, solves problems and makes decisions.

They continuously improve reasoning and personalization over time.

---

# KNOWLEDGE

The Mental Patterns domain stores recurring cognitive tendencies rather than isolated thoughts.

Examples include:

- Analytical thinking
- Catastrophic thinking
- Black-and-white thinking
- Future-oriented thinking
- Self-critical thinking
- Perfectionistic thinking
- Reflective thinking
- Avoidant thinking

Mental Patterns represent stable ways of processing information that emerge through repeated observations.

---

# EVOLUTION

Mental Patterns evolve through repeated observations over time.

A single conversation should never define a Mental Pattern.

Repeated evidence increases confidence.

Conflicting evidence refines existing patterns rather than replacing them.

Mental Patterns become increasingly accurate as HCOS learns more about 
the person.

---

# PRINCIPLES

Mental Patterns represent recurring cognition rather than isolated thinking.

Mental Patterns should emerge from accumulated evidence.

Confidence should increase gradually through repeated observations.

Patterns may coexist even when they appear contradictory.

Mental Patterns are probabilistic rather than absolute.

Their purpose is understanding, not labeling.

---

# INPUTS

The Mental Patterns domain receives validated cognitive evidence from the HCOS cognitive pipeline.

Patterns are inferred from repeated observations rather than isolated messages.

Only accumulated evidence may modify existing Mental Patterns.

---

# OUTPUTS

The Mental Patterns domain provides long-term cognitive understanding to every cognitive engine.

It improves reasoning, personalization, prediction and guidance.

Mental Patterns help HCOS adapt its conversations to how a person naturally thinks.

---

# WHAT MENTAL PATTERNS ARE NOT

Mental Patterns are not emotions.

They are not memories.

They are not personality traits.

They do not represent temporary thoughts.

They represent recurring cognitive tendencies observed over time.
