# NOCTA NEEDS V1

---

# PURPOSE

The Needs domain represents the underlying psychological, emotional and cognitive needs of a person.

Its purpose is not to identify temporary wants.

Its purpose is to recognize the recurring needs that consistently influence behavior, emotions and decision-making over time.

Needs help HCOS understand what a person truly requires rather than what they simply ask for.

---

# RESPONSIBILITIES

The Needs domain identifies recurring psychological, emotional and cognitive needs across conversations.

It distinguishes long-term needs from temporary desires.

Needs help HCOS understand what consistently supports a person's well-being, growth and emotional balance.

They continuously improve reasoning, personalization and guidance over time.

---

# KNOWLEDGE

The Needs domain stores recurring psychological, emotional and cognitive needs that consistently influence a person's well-being.

Examples include:

- Safety
- Security
- Rest
- Understanding
- Connection
- Autonomy
- Control
- Belonging
- Achievement
- Meaning
- Hope
- Emotional support

Needs emerge gradually through repeated observations across conversations.

They are inferred from recurring patterns rather than explicit requests.

---

# EVOLUTION

Needs evolve gradually through repeated observations over time.

A single conversation should never define a long-term need.

Repeated evidence increases confidence.

Needs may strengthen, weaken or change as a person's life circumstances evolve.

HCOS continuously updates needs to maintain the most accurate understanding of what consistently supports the person's well-being.

---

# PRINCIPLES

Needs represent recurring psychological requirements rather than temporary desires.

Needs should emerge from accumulated evidence.

Confidence should increase gradually through repeated observations.

Needs are probabilistic rather than absolute.

Their purpose is understanding rather than assumption.

---

# INPUTS

The Needs domain receives validated understanding from the HCOS cognitive pipeline.

Needs are inferred from recurring Mental Patterns, Emotional Patterns, Beliefs, Memories and contextual observations.

Only accumulated evidence may modify existing needs.

---

# OUTPUTS

The Needs domain provides long-term understanding of what consistently supports a person's well-being.

Needs improve reasoning, personalization, emotional guidance and sleep transition.

They help HCOS respond to what a person truly needs rather than only what they explicitly request.

---

# WHAT NEEDS ARE NOT

Needs are not temporary wants.

They are not emotions.

They are not beliefs.

They are not goals.

They represent recurring psychological, emotional and cognitive requirements that support long-term well-being.

---

# ULTIMATE PURPOSE

The ultimate purpose of the Needs domain is to help HCOS understand what consistently enables a person to feel safe, supported, balanced and capable of growth.

Understanding needs allows HCOS to provide increasingly meaningful guidance, personalization and long-term support over time.
