# NOCTA PREFERENCES V1

---

# PURPOSE

The Preferences domain represents the recurring ways in which a person prefers to interact with HCOS.

Its purpose is not to capture temporary choices.

Its purpose is to recognize stable interaction preferences that improve personalization, communication and guidance over time.

Preferences help HCOS understand how a person prefers to receive support rather than only what support they need.

---

# RESPONSIBILITIES

The Preferences domain identifies recurring interaction preferences across conversations.

It distinguishes stable preferences from temporary choices.

Preferences help HCOS understand how a person prefers to receive communication, guidance and emotional support.

They continuously improve personalization and conversational quality over time.

---

# KNOWLEDGE

The Preferences domain stores recurring interaction preferences that consistently improve the user's experience with HCOS.

Examples include:

- Short responses
- Detailed explanations
- Step-by-step guidance
- Reflective questions
- Direct communication
- Gentle communication
- Logical reasoning
- Emotional reassurance
- Audio guidance
- Silent reflection

Preferences emerge gradually through repeated interactions.

They are inferred from consistent behavior rather than explicit requests.

---

# EVOLUTION

Preferences develop gradually through repeated interactions over time.

A single conversation should never define a long-term preference.

Repeated observations increase confidence.

Preferences may evolve as a person's communication style and needs change.

HCOS continuously updates preferences to maintain the most effective interaction style for each person.

---

# PRINCIPLES

Preferences represent recurring interaction styles rather than temporary choices.

Preferences should emerge from accumulated evidence.

Confidence should increase gradually through repeated observations.

Preferences are probabilistic rather than absolute.

Their purpose is improving communication rather than limiting it.

---

# INPUTS

The Preferences domain receives validated interaction observations from the HCOS cognitive pipeline.

Preferences are inferred from recurring conversational behavior, feedback and contextual interactions.

Only accumulated evidence may modify existing preferences.

---

# OUTPUTS

The Preferences domain provides long-term interaction understanding to every cognitive engine.

Preferences improve personalization, communication style, guidance and emotional support.

They help HCOS deliver responses in the way each person naturally prefers.

---

# WHAT PREFERENCES ARE NOT

Preferences are not needs.

They are not beliefs.

They are not personality traits.

They are not temporary choices.

They represent recurring interaction styles that improve long-term personalization.

---

# ULTIMATE PURPOSE

The ultimate purpose of the Preferences domain is to help HCOS communicate with each person in the most natural, comfortable and effective way.

Understanding preferences enables increasingly personalized conversations while preserving the person's individuality over time.
