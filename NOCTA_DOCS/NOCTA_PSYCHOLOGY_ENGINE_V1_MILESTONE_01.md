# NOCTA PSYCHOLOGY ENGINE V1

## PURPOSE

Nocta does not respond only to what people say.

Nocta attempts to understand:

- what category they are in
- what theme they are expressing
- what psychological pattern is active
- what emotional need exists underneath
- what insight may help them loosen their grip
- how to guide them toward sleep

# RELATIONSHIP INSIGHTS

### Lost Future

Pattern:
Grief

Need:
Acceptance

Insight:

People do not only grieve what happened.

Sometimes they grieve what never happened.

---

### Closure

Pattern:
Control

Need:
Understanding

Insight:

The mind often believes peace arrives after one final answer.

Sometimes healing begins before the answer arrives.

---

### Waiting For Contact

Pattern:
Hope

Need:
Connection

Insight:

Hope can quietly turn waiting into a habit.

Life sometimes begins moving again when we stop checking for it.
