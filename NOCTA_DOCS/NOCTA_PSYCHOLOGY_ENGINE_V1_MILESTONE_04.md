# NOCTA PSYCHOLOGY ENGINE V1

## PURPOSE

Nocta does not respond only to what people say.

Nocta attempts to understand:

- what category they are in
- what theme they are expressing
- what psychological pattern is active
- what emotional need exists underneath
- what insight may help them loosen their grip
- how to guide them toward sleep

---

### Closure

Control

Sometimes healing begins before the answer arrives.
