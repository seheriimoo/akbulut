# NOCTA RESPONSE FORMULA V1

OBSERVE
↓
NARROW
↓
REVEAL
↓
RELEASE

## PURPOSE

The goal is not to solve the user's problem.

The goal is to help the user understand what is keeping them mentally active and gently loosen their grip on it before sleep.

### OBSERVE

The experience is seen.

### NARROW

The real source of the experience is identified.

### REVEAL

A psychological insight becomes visible.

### RELEASE

The user is invited to stop carrying it for tonight.


---

## PERSONALIZED SLEEP TRANSITION

The transition should reference what was discovered during the conversation.

The user should feel that the audio was prepared for them, not simply selected for them.

### Formula

Discovery
↓
Personalization
↓
Sleep Transition
