# NOCTA RESPONSE VALIDATION V1

## PURPOSE

This document validates the quality of Nocta responses.

The goal is not only correct routing.

The goal is emotional impact.

Each response should be evaluated for:

- Warmth
- Human Feel
- Insight Quality
- Reframe Quality
- Sleep Transition Quality

---

# TEST 01

USER MESSAGE:

I can't stop thinking about tomorrow's meeting.

EXPECTED CATEGORY:

Future Anxiety

EXPECTED INSIGHT:

Carrying Tomorrow

EXPECTED RESPONSE:

Tomorrow hasn't arrived yet.

But part of you may already be carrying it.

As if your mind is trying to prepare for every possible outcome before the day even begins.

Maybe what feels heavy tonight is not tomorrow itself.

It may be carrying tomorrow before it exists.

EXPECTED AUDIO:

Future Release

# TEST 06

USER MESSAGE:

EXPECTED RESPONSE:

Regret often asks us to judge yesterday using today's wisdom.

It is proof that you have grown.

# TEST 07

USER MESSAGE:

EXPECTED RESPONSE:

Sometimes exhaustion is not a lack of energy.

It is carrying more than your mind and body were meant to carry without rest.

Not because they are weak.

Because they have been carrying too much for too long.
