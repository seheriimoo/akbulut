# NOCTA SLEEP PROFILES V1

## PURPOSE

This document defines the sleep profiles used by Nocta.

Sleep profiles connect emotional states to sleep experiences.

The goal is not to play a sound.

The goal is to create the most supportive transition into sleep.

## SAFE TOMORROW

Purpose:

Help the user stop carrying tomorrow before it arrives.

Emotional Goal:

Increase feelings of safety and reduce future-focused urgency.

Transition Style:

Grounding

Audio Environment:

Calm ocean waves

Success State:

The user feels that tomorrow can wait until tomorrow.


## QUIET THE MIND

Purpose:

Help the user step out of repetitive thinking loops.

Emotional Goal:

Reduce mental urgency and create space between thoughts.

Transition Style:

Slowing Down

Audio Environment:

Soft rain

Success State:

The user no longer feels the need to solve everything tonight.


## GENTLE LETTING GO

Purpose:

Help the user stop holding on to what cannot be resolved tonight.

Emotional Goal:

Reduce emotional attachment to unresolved relationship thoughts.

Transition Style:

Soft Release

Audio Environment:

Quiet shoreline

Success State:

The user feels less pulled toward the relationship and more present with themselves.


## STILL CONNECTED

Purpose:

Help the user feel less emotionally isolated.

Emotional Goal:

Increase feelings of connection and belonging.

Transition Style:

Gentle Presence

Audio Environment:

Distant night sounds

Success State:

The user feels less alone with their thoughts.


## UNLOAD THE DAY

Purpose:

Help the user put down the mental and emotional weight of the day.

Emotional Goal:

Reduce pressure, responsibility and mental load.

Transition Style:

Decompression

Audio Environment:

Gentle evening ambience

Success State:

The user feels that the day is over and nothing more is required tonight.


## CARRYING LESS

Purpose:

Help the user put down some of the emotional weight they have been carrying.

Emotional Goal:

Create relief without requiring resolution.

Transition Style:

Lightening The Load

Audio Environment:

Quiet open night

Success State:

The user feels that they do not need to carry everything by themselves tonight.
