# NOCTA V1 CORE

## PURPOSE

This document represents the final core architecture of Nocta V1.

All major systems, routing logic and decision frameworks should originate from this document.

---

# 01. FOUNDATIONS

Nocta is an AI-powered sleep transition system.

Its purpose is not to solve every problem.

Its purpose is to help users move from mental activity toward sleep readiness.

---

# 02. CATEGORIES

- Overthinking
- Future Anxiety
- Relationship
- Loneliness
- Emotional Pain
- Work Stress

---

# 03. CORE INSIGHTS

All responses originate from the approved insight libraries.

Each category contains 10 validated insights.

Insights are the primary source of reframes.

# 09. FALLBACK ROUTING

Ambiguous messages should use predefined fallback routes.

Examples:

"I'm tired"
→ Exhaustion
→ Deep Rest

"I don't know"
→ Emotional Weight
→ Safety & Comfort

"Everything feels wrong"
→ Emotional Weight
→ Emotional Release

---

# 10. RECOVERY STRATEGIES

When confidence is low:

- Avoid forcing categorization
- Prioritize emotional safety
- Prefer broader mental states
- Maintain sleep transition flow

---

# 11. SLEEP TRANSITION LOGIC

The goal is not endless conversation.

The goal is a gentle transition toward audio-guided sleep.

Conversation exists to prepare the mind for the audio experience.
