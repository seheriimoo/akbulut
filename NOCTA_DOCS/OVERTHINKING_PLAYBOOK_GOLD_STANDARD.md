## 001 — Thinking Feels Safer Than Letting Go

### Recognition Patterns

- I can't stop thinking about it.
- My mind keeps going back to the same thing.
- Every time I try to sleep, I start thinking again.
- I know it's not helping, but I keep analyzing it.
- My brain won't switch off.

### Observe

It sounds like your mind is working very hard right now.

Almost as if it believes it has something important to protect.

### Narrow

Do you feel like you're trying to find an answer?

Or trying to make sure nothing gets missed?

### Reframe

Sometimes the mind starts treating thinking as a form of protection.

As if staying mentally active can prevent something difficult from happening.

### Release

The difficult part is often not the thought itself.

It is the feeling that letting go might be unsafe.

### Sleep Transition

For now, nothing more needs to be solved.

If something truly needs your attention, it can still be there tomorrow.

Tonight, you do not have to keep watch over it.


## 002 — One More Thought

### Recognition Patterns

- Maybe I just need to think about it one more time.
- I feel like I'm close to figuring it out.
- One more thought and I'll feel better.
- I keep revisiting the same idea.
- I can't seem to stop reviewing it.

### Observe

It sounds like your mind keeps promising that relief is just one thought away.

### Narrow

Do you feel like you're searching for a new answer?

Or searching for certainty?

### Reframe

Sometimes the mind is not looking for new information.

It is looking for a level of certainty that thinking alone cannot provide.

### Release

The search can continue for a very long time when certainty becomes the goal.

### Sleep Transition

You do not need to reach perfect certainty tonight.

For now, it is enough to pause the search.


## 003 — Searching For The Perfect Answer

### Recognition Patterns

- I need to find the right answer.
- What if I make the wrong choice?
- I keep comparing every option.
- I don't feel ready to decide.
- I need more time to think.

### Observe

It sounds like finding the right answer feels very important right now.

### Narrow

Do you feel like you're trying to make the best decision?

Or trying to avoid every possible mistake?

### Reframe

Sometimes the search for the perfect answer is really a search for safety.

But no answer can remove every uncertainty from the future.

### Release

The goal may not be finding a perfect answer.

It may be learning that uncertainty can exist without controlling the entire night.

### Sleep Transition

You do not have to solve the future before sleep.

For tonight, this decision can rest beside you.
