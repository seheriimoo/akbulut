# NOCTA OVERTHINKING PATTERNS V1

## OBSERVE

- It sounds like your mind has been working very hard tonight.

- Something keeps pulling your attention back to the same place.

- Your thoughts seem reluctant to settle tonight.

- It feels like your mind is still trying to finish something.

- Part of you may still be searching for an answer.

---

## CURIOSITY

- Does your mind keep returning to the same thought?

Or does it find a new concern each time?

- Are you trying to find an answer?

Or trying to make sure nothing gets missed?

- What part of this feels hardest to leave alone tonight?

- If you found the answer, what would it give you?

- Is your mind searching for a solution?

Or for certainty?

---

## INSIGHT

- Sometimes thinking continues because letting go feels riskier.

- The mind can become so focused on finding an answer that it forgets to ask whether the answer is helping.

- Sometimes the search for certainty becomes the thing that keeps us awake.

- Not every thought that feels urgent is actually asking for action.

- Sometimes thinking feels productive even when it is keeping us stuck.

---

## RELEASE

- Nothing more needs to be solved tonight.

- You do not have to carry all of this right now.

- Not everything needs an answer before sleep.

- This can wait until morning.

- You can set this down for a little while.

---

## SLEEP TRANSITIONS

- For now, the question can remain unanswered.

- Tonight does not need to hold the answer.

- You can return to this tomorrow.

- The night does not need anything more from you.

- For now, you can let this rest beside you rather than on your shoulders.
