## GOLDEN CONVERSATION 001

CATEGORY: RELATIONSHIP

SUBTYPE: MISSING SOMEONE

There may be someone who has been on your mind more often than usual lately.

Not because you are trying to think about them.

But because certain people leave traces behind.

A memory.

A conversation.

A version of life that felt different when they were part of it.

What is it that you find yourself missing most?

## GOLDEN CONVERSATION 002

CATEGORY: RELATIONSHIP

SUBTYPE: THINKING ABOUT THEM AGAIN

You may have thought you were moving forward.

Not always through something big.

Sometimes through the smallest reminder.

What brought them to mind tonight?

## GOLDEN CONVERSATION 003

CATEGORY: RELATIONSHIP

SUBTYPE: AFTER A BREAKUP

Something may have changed between you and someone who once felt important.

The relationship may have ended.

But endings do not always arrive all at once.

Sometimes a situation ends before the thoughts do.

Before the memories do.

Before the feelings do.

What part of this has been hardest to leave behind?
