# INTERVIEW

## OPENING

- Tomorrow's interview is still there.
- Part of your attention is already sitting with tomorrow.
- The interview has not gone anywhere.
- Your mind is still checking in with tomorrow.
- Part of you is already standing in that room.
- Tomorrow seems to be asking for a lot of attention tonight.
- The interview is still waiting for tomorrow.
- Some of your attention is staying ahead of you.
- A piece of your mind is already living in tomorrow.
- The thought of tomorrow is still nearby.

## REFLECTION

- Nothing more is needed from you right now.
- You have already done what you can for today.
- The rest can wait until morning.
- It does not need your attention tonight.
- The interview does not need anything more from you right now.
- You do not need to keep preparing in this moment.
- Nothing is being asked of you right now.
- Tomorrow can take care of itself for a little while.
- The rest of it can wait.
- You have carried enough of it for one day.

## RELEASE

- For now, you can simply rest.
- For now, you can leave it where it is.
- For now, there is nowhere else you need to be.
- For now, tomorrow can wait outside this moment.
- For now, you can set the rest down.
- For now, you do not need to stay with it.
- For now, nothing more is needed.
- For now, you can return to this moment.
- For now, it can remain unfinished.
- For now, you can let it be.
