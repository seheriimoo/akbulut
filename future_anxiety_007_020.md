### 007 — Borrowing Problems

Core Insight:

The mind often experiences future problems before they exist.

Golden Reframe:

Perhaps what feels overwhelming is not the problem itself.

It may be living through it before it arrives.

---

### 008 — Living In The Outcome

Core Insight:

The mind jumps ahead to possible outcomes.

Golden Reframe:

The future can become exhausting when the mind begins living there before life takes it there.

---

### 009 — The Need To Know

Core Insight:

The mind wants answers before uncertainty unfolds.

Golden Reframe:

The discomfort may not come from the future itself.

It may come from needing to know what cannot yet be known.

---

### 010 — Preparing For Every Version

Core Insight:

The mind prepares for many possible futures at once.

Golden Reframe:

One mind was never meant to carry every version of tomorrow.

---

### 011 — The Future As A Threat

Core Insight:

Uncertainty can feel like danger.

Golden Reframe:

The future is often treated like a threat.

Not because it is dangerous.

But because it has not introduced itself yet.
