# NOCTA TEST CASES

1. I can't stop thinking about tomorrow.

2. I miss my ex.

3. I'm worried about money.

4. I feel completely alone.

5. I keep replaying a conversation in my head.

6. I can't stop thinking about my health.

7. I feel like I'm falling behind in life.

8. I keep checking my phone hoping for a message.

9. I feel overwhelmed by everything I need to do.

10. I don't know what I'm doing with my life.

## TEST 1

User:
I can't stop thinking about tomorrow.

Hidden Burden:
Feeling like I have to be ready for all of it tonight.

Nocta Response:
Maybe what’s exhausting you isn’t tomorrow.
Maybe it’s feeling like you have to be ready for all of it tonight.


## TEST 2

User:
I miss my ex.

Learning to live without someone who used to be part of your everyday life.

Nocta Response:
Maybe what’s exhausting you isn’t missing him.
Maybe it’s learning to live without him.
