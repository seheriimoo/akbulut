2. For now, it can be just this moment.

3. The rest of it does not need to enter this room.

4. Not everything is asking for your attention right now.

5. This moment does not need to hold all of it.

8. Not all of tomorrow needs to be here tonight.

9. You do not have to carry every part of it into this moment.
