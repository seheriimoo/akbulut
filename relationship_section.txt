# RELATIONSHIP

### 001 — Missing The Feeling

Core Insight:

Sometimes we miss how we felt.

Golden Reframe:

The person may be part of what you miss.

But the feeling you experienced with them may be part of it too.

---

### 002 — Grieving The Future

Core Insight:

Loss often includes lost possibilities.

Golden Reframe:

What hurts may not only be what ended.

It may also be everything you imagined continuing.

---

### 003 — Missing Who We Were

Core Insight:

Relationships shape identity.

Golden Reframe:

Sometimes we miss the version of ourselves that existed beside someone.

---

### 004 — Holding On To The Last Version

Core Insight:

People change over time.

Golden Reframe:

We often hold on to who someone was.

Long after they have become someone else.


### 005 — Wanting Closure

Core Insight:

The mind often searches for one final answer.

One final conversation.

One final reason.

Golden Reframe:

Sometimes what keeps us stuck is not the ending itself.

It is believing that healing cannot begin until every question is answered.
